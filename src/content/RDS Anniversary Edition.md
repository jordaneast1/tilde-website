<p>To celebrate ten years since RÜFÜS DU SOL’s debut album Atlas, Tilde Visual created the 3D assets for the vinyl cover designs of the 10 Year Anniversary Edition – a limited 3LP box set pressed on white and blue vinyl, bringing together the full album, a selection of the band’s favourite remixes and a booklet of never-before-seen photos. Our 3D work helped reimagine the world of Atlas for a new generation of listeners, revisiting the record that started it all with a fresh, contemporary finish.</p>
