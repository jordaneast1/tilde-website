<p><b>Real-time visuals for RÜFÜS DU SOL’s three-hour DJ set on Coachella’s brand-new Quasar stage – Weekend 2, April 2024.</b></p>

<p>In 2024 Coachella launched Quasar, a new stage built for extended three-to-four-hour sets from some of the biggest names in electronic music. Its design centres on two monolithic, angled LED walls that frame the Coachella landscape and transform with each artist who takes the stage. For RÜFÜS DU SOL’s first set on Quasar, Tilde Visual created the visual show for the trio’s three-hour DJ set.</p>

<p>Rather than playing back pre-rendered video, the Quasar stage runs live in Unreal Engine. We built our content in Unreal Engine to run in real time alongside Prismax’s custom Unreal Engine build – the large-scale live music platform developed by Tomorrowland’s long-time visual partner. Working natively in the engine meant our scenes could live inside the stage’s real-time environment, respond to live triggers as the set unfolded, and be tuned on site rather than re-rendered, giving the show the flexibility to follow the music across three hours.</p>

<p>The visuals drew on RÜFÜS DU SOL’s visual language – the elements, cinematic abstracts and soft gradients – and carried through content from their Zamna show. Every scene was designed around the bespoke shape of the stage’s LED sculpture, with worlds playing on gradated streams, fluted glass and shuffling steps, building from golden hour into night.</p>
